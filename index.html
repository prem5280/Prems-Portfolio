About

15+ years turning rollout plans into networks that hold up in production.

I'm a self-motivated, result-oriented telecom professional with a track record in solution design and deployment across GSM, UMTS, LTE, 5G and NR technologies, spanning telecom, IT infrastructure, and managed services.

My work sits at the intersection of design and delivery — building effective solutions for telecom deployment, transitioning multi-year projects, and ensuring seamless end-to-end service. I lead with Agile methodology to keep teams shipping high-quality rollouts within deadline, and I stay hands-on with new technologies as they arrive.

"Every network swap is judged by one thing: how little the customer notices it happened."

Beyond delivery, I've trained customers, partners, and team members on GSM, LTE and 5G technologies, managed subcontractor and vendor relationships, and kept live networks running at up to 99% uptime with minimum MTTR.
